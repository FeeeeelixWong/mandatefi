# Brand - MandateFi

_Status: active_

## Positioning

MandateFi is an owner-controlled AI portfolio manager for PancakeSwap. It should feel like a serious DeFi workspace: direct, legible, energetic, and precise.

## Visual Direction

- Use PancakeSwap-inspired purple as the primary action color, cyan for information, mint for confirmed execution, amber for risk, and coral only for destructive actions.
- Prefer flat surfaces with one-pixel borders. Cards use an 8px radius or less.
- Keep the page focused on portfolio state, strategy composition, action queues, and policy limits.
- Use Inter for interface text and Kanit for display headings and financial values.

## Voice

- Explain what the AI will do, why, and what it is not allowed to do.
- Never imply guaranteed returns or present model assumptions as live market data.
- Label execution coverage explicitly: live, approval required, or planned adapter.
